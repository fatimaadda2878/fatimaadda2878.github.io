02 / 07
Machine Learning · Bioinformatics · Project Management

Predicting protein solubility before starting production.

The challenge: in R&D, a construct that looks promising on paper may end up in inclusion bodies. The problem often becomes apparent only after days of cell culture, purification and analysis—consuming time and resources.

My solution: an online tool where a scientist enters a protein sequence to obtain a solubility score, a PDB/UniProt identification when a reliable match is available, and a reasoned plan of expression and purification experiments to compare.

My end-to-end approach

01

Problem framing

User needs, success criteria and the cost of errors.

02

Data

62,478 DeepSol sequences; MMseqs2 was used to group similar sequences and reduce leakage across dataset splits.

03

Modeling

Compared LightGBM and ESM-2 (35M, 150M, 650M), then partially fine-tuned ESM-2 150M with 13 biochemical descriptors.

04

Deployment

Gradio app hosted on Hugging Face Spaces, automated GitHub Actions tests and restricted model weights.

05

Decision-making

Selected a threshold based on business costs: discarding a viable protein was assigned twice the cost of an unnecessary experiment.

→
